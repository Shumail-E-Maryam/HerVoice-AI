# HerVoice AI — AI Ethics & Responsible AI

## Privacy

HerVoice is designed around data minimization.

Users are not required to provide:

- name
- email
- phone number
- password
- government identification

The application uses an anonymous browser session identifier to maintain
conversation continuity.

## User Autonomy

HerVoice should support users in understanding situations and exploring
options without making personal decisions for them.

The system should not pressure, shame, manipulate, or blame users.

## AI Limitations

HerVoice is an AI system and may produce incorrect or incomplete
information.

Users should verify important medical, legal, financial, or safety
decisions with qualified professionals.

## Grounded Information

Resource-related responses are restricted to verified knowledge supplied
to the system.

The system should not invent:

- organizations
- helplines
- phone numbers
- laws
- procedures
- websites

## Safety

Potentially high-risk situations are handled through a dedicated safety
layer before normal conversational generation.

## Language Accessibility

HerVoice supports English and Urdu, including natural Roman Urdu input
where appropriate.

## Transparency

HerVoice clearly communicates that it is an AI support and information
tool rather than a human professional.

## Human Oversight

AI responses should not be treated as a replacement for qualified
professional judgment in high-stakes situations.

## Future Improvements

Future production versions should consider:

- stronger multilingual safety models
- formal red-team testing
- external privacy/security review
- managed production database infrastructure
- expanded verified resource coverage
- continuous evaluation
