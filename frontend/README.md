# 🌸 HerVoice AI

### Your voice. Your choices. Your space.

HerVoice AI is a privacy-focused conversational support and information
platform designed to give users a space to talk, understand situations,
explore options, find verified information, and create practical plans.

---

## ✨ Features

- Anonymous browser-based sessions
- Persistent conversations
- English and Urdu support
- Roman Urdu support
- Multiple conversation modes
- Just Listen
- Help Me Understand
- Explore My Options
- Find Information
- Help Me Make a Plan
- Safety detection
- Verified resource retrieval
- SQLite conversation persistence
- Groq-powered conversational AI
- FastAPI backend
- React + TypeScript frontend
- Responsive interface
- MCP-oriented resource discovery
- Evaluation scripts

## 🔐 Privacy

HerVoice does not require a name, email, password, or phone number.

An anonymous browser-generated session identifier is used to restore
conversations within the same browser.

Clearing browser storage or changing devices may create a new anonymous
session.

---

## ⚠️ Important Disclaimer

HerVoice AI is not a therapist, doctor, lawyer, emergency service, or
replacement for qualified professional support.

AI-generated information can be incomplete or incorrect.
